# 📈 Subscriber Acquisition & Congestion-Based Dynamic Pricing Optimization

## 📌 1. Business Problem Statement
> *"Acquiring as many subscribers as possible remains a critical goal. In recent years, the number of users has been growing extremely fast and pricing emerged as a tool to limit congestion and increase revenue at the same time."*

---

## 💡 2. Context & Problem Identification
* **The Issue:** Rapid subscriber growth leads to network congestion during peak hours, causing service degradation. Standard static pricing fails to balance network traffic loads or capture optimal revenue from high-demand periods.
* **The Solution:** Implement dynamic pricing and demand-forecasting models to balance subscriber acquisition goals with network bandwidth capacities, using price elasticity to smooth out peak congestion while maximizing overall average revenue per user (ARPU).

---

## 📊 3. Business Objectives & Constraints

### 🎯 Business Objectives
* **Maximize Subscriber Acquisition Rate:** Maintain aggressive market share expansion and new user onboarding.
* **Maximize Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** Optimize tariff plans and peak-hour pricing tiers to increase revenue streams.
* **Minimize Peak Network Congestion:** Use dynamic pricing incentives to shift non-essential data traffic to off-peak hours.

### ⚠️ Constraints & Limitations
* **Minimize Subscriber Churn & Price Sensitivity Risk:** Ensure price adjustments do not cause customer dissatisfaction or drive users to competitors.
* **Minimize Regulatory Compliance Violations:** Maintain strict adherence to local telecom tariff regulations and consumer protection laws regarding pricing transparency.

---

## 👥 4. Stakeholder Expectations
* **Revenue & Pricing Strategy Team:** Require clear data-driven elasticities to design tiered tariff plans and off-peak promotions.
* **Network Planning & Capacity Team:** Expect managed peak load traffic to avoid costly emergency infrastructure expansions.
* **Marketing & Sales Teams:** Require competitive onboarding offers to keep customer acquisition velocity high.

---

## ⚙️ 5. Feasibility Assessment
* **Technical Feasibility:** High. Predictive econometric modeling, price elasticity algorithms, and real-time load forecasting models effectively balance price adjustments against usage metrics.
* **Data Feasibility:** High. Telecom systems continuously log call detail records (CDRs), tower traffic loads, data consumption rates, and billing transactions.

---

## 🚀 7. Business Impact
Harmonizing subscriber acquisition with intelligent, congestion-aware pricing allows telecom operators to scale their user base sustainably, maximize infrastructure ROI, and significantly improve top-line revenue.
